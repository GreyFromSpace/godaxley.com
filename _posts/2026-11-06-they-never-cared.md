That's the way I feel right now. These friends of mine have been with me since high school, K since day one and J not long after, and in one day they fully disappointed me to the point I felt the need to cut them fully out. I was so angry, so hurt. Now they won't talk to me...

I hate myself so much. I really do.

It started when they came by to help me move from the shitty fifth wheel I was living in, a mildly sketchy handshake agreement to rent the thing while I looked for better living situations. I was willing to take ANYTHING over the streets, living out of my honda CR-V. A 2012 model with the 2015 GPS.

Living as a homeless man in a car is actually not as bad as I thought it would be. it most certainly was hell on wheels, much more literally for me than others.

I was miserable during that time. I remember sobbing in my boyfriend's arms, my big fluffy lion-bear... He held me as I wet his shoulder, my tears creating a damp spot he ignored completely... I don't deserve him. I really don't and god am I so grateful he chooses to tolerate my bullshit...

They used to tolerate my bullshit too, I guess I wore their patience thin. I wear all my relationships thin, much like socks or the inner thighs of every pair of underwear i've ever had, every sole of every shoe, every old credit card I obsessively bent in half over and over until it finally snapped clean...

Yeah, I choke out every friendship i've ever had and now here's the proof! The oldest friends i've ever had in my entire life and they left me because I was too much, because I am too broken.

Anyway, i've digressed for several line breaks now. The move took days. J had been helping me clean and pack for days, and I was paying them both for their time. The 16th was supposed to be the final push. So they showed up to help me move, and immediately both of these friends, I'll call them K and J for privacy's sake, were arguing past each other about the logistics of the task. One was saying "I don't want to," the other was saying "but we can." One was arguing for what they had the energy for, the other was arguing for what was possible, which if they'd stopped to listen they'd have realized they were arguing two different things. One was trying to get the most done in the least time, the other was trying to budget their spoons.

I was in the middle, sleep deprived from yet another sleepless anxiety spiral filled night as I was NOT ready to deal with moving and the excitement of it all was just far too much for me to take... All my clean clothes had already gone to the new place. I literally did not sleep because I smelled like ass and had no way to fix it, and I don't like my friends being forced to be near me when I smell bad. So I let them argue, went outside, called my boyfriend, my sweet bear-lion man. No answer, no text. I try again. No answer. He's busy, which is fine but I needed him so bad in that moment. To ground me, to tell me it was okay, to give me the time away from the stress enough to just make it through. He didn't fail, it was never his fault. I wasn't mad or upset but I was a bit shaky.

I went back inside, they were still fighting, it had been long enough that i'd about had it, I was out of energy, on the verge of tears. I could only utter "I need you guys to leave... get out..." it came out harsh... I started to cry, "I'm sorry, please. We can do this another day, I need you gone..."

J's reaction was perfect. I was very thankful with how calmly they recognized I was overwhelmed. K on the other hand decided that my reaction was uncalled for, and got uppy with me while I was breaking down. They both left, K loudly exclaiming that none of that was their fault. Classic K arrogance that i'd grown fond of, now suddenly soured for me in mere seconds. I couldn't really move, I sat on the corner of my bed facing away from them, I couldn't even look, I couldn't bear it.

I texted them both after. To J: "you're not obligated to help anymore, your payment will be on payday. I will figure this out myself." To K: "Your payment will be on payday, like the 20th. thank you. Sorry for wasting your time."

J wrote back: "Don't pay me to help you move at this point, that's too stressful for me." And later: "I really lost my temper earlier... But you didn't do anything wrong, you did the right thing to ask us to leave and I respect it, it's okay."

I cried for a while until I went numb, then I slept. I slept until it was over. Then I slept until it was dark, then I just stared, for a long time. The whole plan for the day was gone, and I was stuck without a computer for all of it. I felt like i'd lost my friends that day and I hadn't, not yet.

That night I sent K one more text: "how much time have you been spending talking to Ai? I am curious."

J came back to help the next day with a truck, and so did my lovely Lion-Bear. They made everything okay that next day. That week I also found out J and K were splitting up. J had told K they wanted to just be friends.

Everything was okay, that is, until I just had to pick the scab while it was still fresh.

Two days later, K answered my AI question. "you might be projecting a little." I let that go and asked what I actually wanted to ask: what the fuck is going on, man? What's your deal? I brought up the time they criticized me in the middle of lunch with my boyfriend, in public. I brought up the argument in my home, and them getting uppy with me when I broke down. I told them they can't have it one way, where i'm the one who always needs to be parented by them. And I told them I was genuinely worried about them.

K apologized. Twice. For the restaurant, and for how they reacted when I told them to leave. "Forgiven and accepted," I said, and I meant it.

Then: "but i _really_ don't appreciate having my time wasted." The argument "wasn't a big deal." I "could have just as easily asked for space." They "will not accept fault there."

I tried to explain. Setting the boundary at all was terrifying. Asking them to leave at all took so much effort, it was akin to moving three tons with my toe. If they'd just sat in the truck and finished up, then came back, I would have been ok.

"i'm not going to accept full responsibility because you want to justify your response because of trauma. and your trauma response is the reason your time was wasted."

I asked them to calmly re-read what they just said and ask themself if they'd like to continue having Grey as a friend.

"if you're going to give me an ultimatum over something as petty then no, not really." "what i said was clear and factual."

"Okay. That's that then. I guess we grew apart. That really sucks."

They sent back an eye-roll emoji.

I told them exactly what I thought of them. "You're a fucking child. Grow up. Please never come around me again." I blocked them. Then I unblocked them just to say "I'm dead fucking serious, k. Do not ever come around me again," and blocked them again.

In my emotional outburst I moved my pieces into the perfect space, and they took all my checkers. I get it now. I already accepted my parts here. It's the dismissal of my experience and feelings that leave the balances tilted for me, and that hurt... the fact they were so easily willing to be so callous.

It's distance, but i'm still feeling like i'm losing my oldest friend.

I sent J the screenshots and asked them to judge. AITA? The next morning I told J, "i'm not going to pretend that I handled any of that well." J gave me their take, and then said they didn't want to be brought into things between me and K anymore, and that one of my replies was shady and snarky. "I didn't need the shrapnel of your conversation with [K]." Then they muted the chat so I could choose what I wanted to say.

I told them it was clear whose side they'd taken. Then: "ok. I love you. I just need space. A lot of space. For a while."

I sent K gas money I'd promised. Then I blocked J too.

K had run over a fire pit at the old place. Somebody had to replace it, and people were looking at me to fix it. I asked J to help sort it out. Nobody answered. Today I paid for it myself, even though K agreed it wasn't my responsibility. I'm broke for the rest of the month.

And today I unblocked them both and lashed out. I told J they both threw me away the second I became a burden. I told K I hate them.

What the hell did I think that would accomplish?
