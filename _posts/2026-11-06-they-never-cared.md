They never cared... That's the way I feel right now. These friends of mine were with me since day one of high school and in one day they fully disappointed me to the point I had to cut them fully out. I was so angry, so hurt. Now they won't talk to me...

I hate myself so much. I really do.

It started when they came by to help me move from the shitty fifth wheel I was living in, a mildly sketchy handshake agreement to rent the thing while I looked for better living situations. I was willing to take ANYTHING over the streets, living out of my honda CR-V. A 2012 model with the 2015 GPS.

Living as a homeless man in a car is actually not as bad as I thought it would be. it most certainly was hell on wheels, much more literally for me than others.

I was miserable during that time. I remember sobbing in my boyfriend's arms, my big fluffy lion-bear... He held me as I wet his shoulder, my tears creating a damp spot he ignored completely... I don't deserve him. I really don't and god am I so grateful he chooses to tolerate my bullshit...
